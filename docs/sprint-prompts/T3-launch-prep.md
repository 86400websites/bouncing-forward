## Deviations and learnings

- **Structured tags are invisible to the title.** Playwright's `tag:` option is not part of `testInfo.title` or `--list` text; only `testInfo.tags` and the JSON reporter carry it, and the reporter strips the `@`. Any guard or grep on the title alone silently matches nothing. Both sides now normalise.
- **IN-010 needed a Production branch** before it could serve as a morning check: the live route deliberately returns five fields, not sixteen.
- **Three judgement calls made and flagged, not asked:** both "Last updated" dates advanced to 11 September 2026 because Terms §11 promises it; the pages were left noindexed because the owner had not asked to lift it and re-indexing is awkward to undo; Heather's apparent typo "website site" was normalised to "website" rather than reproduced.
- **The owner declined the deliberate-failure test of the alert channel** ("No need to deliberately fail as its all working"). The morning check's success run proves the workflow; the failure email path itself is **unverified** and is recorded as such.
- **Clarification on the reviewer's Should-fix (recorded 11 September, after Codex compared the source with Heather's email).** The finding that the Privacy introduction still says Bouncing Forward "is run by Maher Kaddoura and is part of Half a Life" is an **optional editorial clarification, not a failure to implement Heather's instructions** — her email did not ask for that introduction to change, and every edit she did ask for is implemented and live. The historical review record in `docs/code-reviews/T3-launch-prep-review.md` is preserved as returned; this note only settles its disposition.
- **What this sprint does not establish:** that the settled copy is legally sufficient for any jurisdiction. It establishes that Heather's instructions were implemented as given and are live. Legal adequacy is outside the gate's scope and remains the owner's.
